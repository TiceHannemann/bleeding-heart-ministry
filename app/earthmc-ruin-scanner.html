Feed source
      
      
Positions come from the live server map: every player it shows, refreshed every few seconds. Each position is matched to the claimed chunk under it. A visit is logged when someone stands in a town that isn't theirs and isn't in their nation. Players can be hidden from the live map, so not seeing someone proves nothing. On the Ministry server, visits are recorded around the clock; opened as a file, this page records while it is open.

How it learns
Every visit gets a reconnaissance score from how the visitor behaved: time along the edge of the claims instead of at the town spawn, a wandering path, several towns of one nation in a day, return visits, and whose side they are on. Mark visits as Recon, Trade, Passing or Friendly and the model learns what scouting looks like on this server.

At each scan, every pair of nations with real traffic in the last day gets a Forecast Lab forecast: will the visitors' nation make a hostile move against the host within seven days (declare enmity, break an alliance, take a town, or cut land where they visited)? The event log checks the outcome, and the model adjusts.

How it works: after every scan each model gives a probability for its event, using what the archive shows about that town or nation. When the event happens, or its window closes, a later scan checks the event log and the model adjusts its weights toward the real outcome. Models start from the same rules as the rest of the Ministry, so “vs rules” shows whether learning has helped. Automatic scans keep it learning around the clock.
